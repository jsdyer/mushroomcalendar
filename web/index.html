How to read this notebook

A calendar of observations,
not a census of fungi.

Rain, access, observer effort, popular parks and identification difficulty all affect this picture. One organism can have several observations. A quiet month may mean fewer observers, not fewer fungi. A missing record does not establish absence.

All three completed years are shown together by default. Weekly charts use ISO week numbers, including week 53; year filters use the observation's calendar year. Obscured records keep month-level dates only and are excluded from weekly charts and the map.

Coverage & freshness

    The date range stays fixed at 2023–2025 so whole seasons are comparable. The collector rechecks weekly for new uploads, identification edits and deletions. A retained capture is the dataset as seen on that date, not the date the fungi appeared.

    Sources, attribution & reuse

    Observation facts come from the iNaturalist public API. Each row credits its contributor and licence and links to the source. Only CC0, CC BY and CC BY-NC observation licences are included; other licences and unlicensed records are outside coverage. Photos are not copied.

    Reuse must follow each observation's licence, including attribution and noncommercial restrictions where applicable. The exported CSV includes the contributor, licence and observation ID. Software is MIT licensed; that does not relicense the observations.

    API practices · Data quality explained · Geoprivacy explained

    Location privacy & browser privacy

    We never request private coordinates. Open public coordinates are rounded to a 0.02° grid before saving. Obscured or private observations have no saved coordinates or exact day. The map is a geographic grid, with no background tiles; it is not suitable for finding individual organisms.

    Filtering and CSV creation happen in your browser. Your filters are not stored or sent to iNaturalist. The page downloads snapshots from this site. Opening source links visits their providers. CoolCode records aggregate page opens without filter values.
